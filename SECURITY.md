## 🔒 The Kryptonite Rule
At **SynaptoBot AI**, security is absolute. Our core operational philosophy—known as the **Kryptonite Rule**—enforces strict, uncompromised boundaries to protect user assets and proprietary infrastructure:

1. **Non-Custodial Absolute Control:** User funds and staking operations remain strictly non-custodial. The platform interacts via secure Solana smart contracts, eliminating third-party counterparty vulnerability.
2. **Rigorous Stress-Testing:** No trading strategy or execution model is deployed without surviving extreme **Monte Carlo stress-testing** and automated **StrategyQuant X** validation pipelines.
3. **Strict Data & Infrastructure Isolation:** Proprietary algorithmic weights, core MQL5 execution engines, and private database credentials are fully encrypted and segregated from public telemetry.
