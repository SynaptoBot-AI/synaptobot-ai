## 🤝 Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the SynaptoBot AI community a harassment-free and professional experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, or religion.

## 💡 Standards of Behavior
* **Professionalism & Respect:** Engage constructively, communicate clearly, and maintain professional courtesy across all official channels (Discord, GitHub, X).
* **Security Awareness:** Respect platform guidelines, avoid sharing unverified or malicious links, and adhere to operational security best practices.
* **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus discussions on architectural improvements, documentation clarity, and ecosystem growth.

## 🛑 Enforcement
The core maintainers of SynaptoBot AI reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
