# Project Roadmap

This is a list of possible work areas, not a delivery schedule or commitment. Priorities and timing depend on testing, infrastructure, security, legal review, and available resources.

## Current focus

- Improve reliability and clarity of the website, API, and Discord bot prototypes.
- Validate market-data ingestion, timestamps, symbol handling, and alert behavior against source data.
- Evaluate the Synapto AI / NEXUS assistant for response quality, latency, resource use, and safe handling of financial questions.
- Publish clear product status, privacy, risk, and usage information before inviting a wider test group.

## Later, subject to review

- A limited, opt-in public beta if operational and privacy checks pass.
- Solana features only after product, security, legal, and operational requirements are defined and independently reviewed.
- Optional paid access only after pricing, support, refund, privacy, tax, and applicable regulatory requirements have been resolved.

## Explicitly not announced

There is no committed Mainnet launch date, token sale, DEX liquidity pool, staking or rewards launch, exchange listing, automated trading launch, or Founder Pass sale. Do not treat interface mockups or earlier aspirational dates as a promise.
